Sky typeface family
====================

This font is derived from Roboto, Copyright Google Inc.,
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Modifications made from the original Roboto source:
- Renamed family and internal font metadata ("Sky")
- Verified against a "Gold Standard" type specification combining:
  Helvetica's structural neutrality and stroke discipline,
  Frutiger's open apertures and ergonomic legibility,
  Roboto's screen-rendering engineering discipline.
  (Full spec: Gold-Standard-Blueprint.docx)
- Measured analysis confirmed Roboto's x-height ratio (74.3% of
  cap-height), aperture openness on c/e (42-44 degrees), R leg
  angle, G spur construction, double-story a, and numeral (1/3/6)
  construction already meet or exceed the specification's targets,
  so no structural glyph edits were needed on top of the verified base.
